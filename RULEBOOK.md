# ABYSS INC. — OFFICIAL GAME RULEBOOK

**Subtitle:** *The Ocean Is Not for Sale*  
**Version:** 3.1.0  
**Genre:** Single-Player 3D Environmental Strategy & Interactive Adventure

---

## 1. Game Title and Central Objective

**ABYSS INC. — The Ocean Is Not for Sale** is a single-player environmental strategy and interactive 3D adventure game.  
The central objective is to establish a deep-sea mining company, extract minerals to meet your business target, witness and manage the ecological consequences of industrial extraction, make two permanent company rule changes, clean floating surface oil pollution, restore the damaged underwater coral reef, and continue operating your company across repeatable surface-and-seabed expeditions.

**Core Message:** *You can change the rules of your business, but you cannot change the laws of nature.*

---

## 2. Story Introduction

You begin aboard an industrial research-and-mining vessel on the open ocean surface alongside **Captain Harlan**. You name yourself and your company, set initial business parameters, and lower your compact mining crawler (**TRITON-IX**) to the seabed. As you extract minerals, sediment clouds spread, coral reefs lose color, and oil waste rises to the ocean surface. Completing the initial story unlocks **Ongoing Operations**, allowing you to freely dive, surface, mine remaining deposits, sell ore, upgrade equipment, and protect the ocean.

---

## 3. Single-Player Role & Advisory

You play as the **Company Founder & Operations Director**, controlling the surface vessel, the underwater mining crawler, the reef-restoration diver, and all business and policy decisions. **Captain Harlan** and the ship's **Marine Science Advisory** provide contextual guidance and ecological alerts.

## 5. Gameplay Stages & Ongoing Operations

1. **Stage 1 — Ocean Surface (Welcome Aboard):** Name your player and company on the ocean surface.
2. **Stage 2 — Initial Business Settings:** Set Machine Capacity, Ore Selling Price, and Extraction Target.
3. **Stage 3 — First Deployment:** Watch the boat's A-frame crane lower the mining machine to the seabed.
4. **Stage 4 — Underwater Mining & Rule Change 1:** Extract ore from finite deposits while observing seabed damage, then enact **Rule Change 1**.
5. **Stage 5 — Surface Cleanup:** Return to the surface (`[F]`) and steer the boat's skimmer boom to collect organic floating oil slicks. (You can also press `[F]` at any time to dive back to the seabed and return repeatedly.)
6. **Stage 6 — Rule Change 2 & Restoration Budget:** Enact **Rule Change 2** governing mineral sales and allocate a Small, Standard, or Generous restoration budget.
7. **Stage 7 — Reef Restoration:** Complete **Mini-Game A (Clean the Reef)** and **Mini-Game B (Water-Flow Logic Puzzle + Coral Placement)**.
8. **Stage 8 — Tutorial Complete → Ongoing Operations:** Click **CONTINUE OPERATIONS** to remain in your persistent world—freely diving (`[F]`) and surfacing (`[F]`), mining remaining seabed deposits (`240t` total reserves), selling ore, buying equipment upgrades (`+15t Hold`, `+25% Drill`), cleaning new oil slicks, and maintaining reef health.

---

## 6. Three Initial Business Settings

*(These are starting parameters and do **not** count as the two in-campaign dynamic rule changes.)*

1. **Mining Machine Capacity (`20t`, `35t`, or `50t`):** Determines how much ore the machine can hold before offloading.
2. **Ore Selling Price (`$2,000/t` to `$4,500/t`):** Higher prices increase potential revenue, but asking above `$3,500/t` carries commercial buyer pushback (up to a 32% negotiated discount).
3. **Extraction Target (`40t` to `100t`):** Sets your company's initial ore extraction goal out of `240t` finite seabed reserves.

---

## 7. The Two In-Campaign Dynamic Rule Changes

Each rule change is permanent for the campaign and genuinely alters gameplay conditions:

### Rule Change 1 — The Extraction Rule *(Triggered after initial mining & environmental warning)*
- **Option A: Continuous Extraction** — Permits continued mining immediately, but increases pollution (`1.25x` disturbance).
- **Option B: Restoration-First Permit** — Prohibits further mining until surface cleanup and reef restoration are completed, protecting the reef from further damage.

### Rule Change 2 — The Mineral Sales Rule *(Triggered after surface oil cleanup)*
- **Option A: Open Market** — Allows immediate sale of extracted ore without environmental certification at standard market price.
- **Option B: Eco-Certified Market** — Blocks mineral sales until surface cleanup and reef restoration are completed, then grants a **+35% Eco-Certified premium**.

---

## 8. Immutable Ecological Laws

Regardless of any business setting or rule change:
1. Mining always produces environmental disturbance and sediment plumes.
2. Sediment damages the seabed and nearby coral habitats.
3. Mineral reserves on the ocean floor are strictly finite (`240t` across 8 veins).
4. Pollution cannot be removed by changing financial settings.
5. Restoration requires direct player interaction and effort.
6. Extracted minerals cannot be recreated simply by cleaning the ocean.

---

## 9. Repeatable Surface & Underwater Navigation

- While on the **Surface**, press **`F`** (or click **⬇️ DIVE TO OCEAN FLOOR [F]**) to lower the mining machine and transition to the seabed.
- While **Underwater**, press **`F`** (or click **⬆️ RETURN TO SURFACE [F]**) to safely stop mining, offload collected ore to the boat stockpile, and ascend to the surface vessel.
- All company state, inventory, credits, depleted deposits, cleaned pollution, reef health, and enacted rules are preserved across every transition.

---

## 10. Winning, Ongoing Play, and Defeat Conditions

- **Tutorial Completion & Ongoing Operations:** Completing both rule decisions, surface cleanup, and reef restoration displays the **TUTORIAL COMPLETE!** screen. Selecting **CONTINUE OPERATIONS** keeps you in the active game indefinitely.
- **Defeat — Bankruptcy:** Triggered if Company Credits fall below `$0`, or below `$25K` during budget allocation with no sellable ore.
- **Defeat — Ecological Collapse:** Triggered if Global Ocean Health drops to `10%` or below.

---

## 11. Optional Between-Run Constitution System

Accessible from the setup screen or upon defeat, players may ratify up to 2 permanent **Between-Run Charter Amendments** (`ConstitutionPersistence.js`). This optional meta-progression is separate from the 2 mandatory in-campaign dynamic rule changes.
