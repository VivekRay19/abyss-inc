# ABYSS INC. — OFFICIAL GAME RULEBOOK

**Subtitle:** *The Ocean Is Not for Sale*  
**Version:** 3.4.0  
**Genre:** Single-Player 3D Environmental Strategy & Interactive Adventure

---

## 1. Game Title and Central Objective

**ABYSS INC. — The Ocean Is Not for Sale** is a single-player environmental strategy and interactive 3D adventure game.  
The central objective is to establish a deep-sea mining company, configure four starting parameters, extract minerals toward your target, manage the ecological consequences of industrial extraction, make up to two permanent company rule changes, clean floating surface oil pollution, and restore varied coral reef sanctuaries.

**Core Message:** *You can change the rules of your business, but you cannot change the laws of nature.*

---

## 2. Starting Company Credits & Four Functional Starting Parameters

Every new company receives a fixed **Starting Company Credits Grant of `$120,000` (`$120K`)**.  
Before launching, you select **four starting parameters** (separate from the two in-campaign dynamic rule changes):

1. **Mining Machine Capacity (`20T`, `35T`, or `50T`):** Determines how much ore the mining machine (`TRITON-IX`) can carry per trip.
2. **Workshop Capacity (`50T`, `100T`, or `150T`):** Determines how much extracted ore can be stored at the boat's workshop before it must be sold.
3. **Ore Selling Price (`$2,000/T` to `$4,500/T`):** Determines the actual credits earned per tonne when minerals are sold from the workshop.
4. **Extraction Target (`40T` to `120T`):** Determines your extraction objective and the progress (`Ore Extracted: X / YT`) displayed during gameplay. Seabed mineral reserves (`Deposit Remaining: 240T` across 8 veins) are tracked separately.

## 3. Simplified Controls

### Underwater Screen
- **Move Mining Machine:** `W` `A` `S` `D` or Arrow Keys
- **`E` — Mine Ore:** Drill and collect ore when near a glowing mineral deposit.
- **`F` — Return to Surface:** Safely stop mining, offload ore into the boat's Workshop (up to `Workshop Capacity`), and ascend to the surface.

### Surface Screen
- **Steer Boat:** `W` `A` `S` `D` or Arrow Keys
- **`E` — Skim Oil:** Collect floating oil slicks into the skimmer tank.
- **`R` — Empty Tank:** Empty the skimmer collection tank.
- **`F` — Dive to Ocean Floor:** Lower the mining machine back to the seabed at any time.
- **Restore Reef:** Open the Reef Restoration screen (includes a **`← Back`** button to return without losing progress).

---

## 4. The Two In-Campaign Dynamic Rule Changes (Max 2)

You may enact a maximum of **two rule changes** during a campaign (either when prompted by story events or by clicking a `Pending` rule badge in the top bar):

### Rule Change 1 — The Extraction Rule
- **Continuous Extraction:** Permits continued mining immediately, with `1.25x` seabed disturbance.
- **Restoration-First Permit:** Prohibits further mining until surface cleanup and reef restoration are completed.

### Rule Change 2 — The Mineral Sales Rule
- **Open Market:** Allows immediate sale of Workshop ore at your selected Ore Selling Price.
- **Eco-Certified Market:** Locks mineral sales until surface cleanup and reef restoration are completed, then grants a **+35% Eco-Certified premium**.

---

## 5. Reef Restoration, Puzzle Variety & Varied Coral Outcomes

Opening **Restore Reef** lets you inspect the current restoration task or return via **`← Back`** without losing progress. Each restoration cycle selects from a shuffled deck of **4 solvable water-flow puzzle variations**, each restoring a distinct 3D coral formation on the seabed:
1. **Variation 1 (Northern Ridge Conduit):** Restores *Crimson Staghorn & Golden Antler Thicket*.
2. **Variation 2 (Deep Trench Thermal Siphon):** Restores *Bioluminescent Azure & Violet Sea Fans*.
3. **Variation 3 (Outer Atoll Shelf Circulation):** Restores *Emerald Brain Dome & Organ-Pipe Sanctuary*.
4. **Variation 4 (Cascading Terrace Channels):** Restores *Sunburst Table-Coral & Lavender Rosette Reef*.

---

## 6. Tutorial Completion & Ongoing Operations

Completing the initial story displays **TUTORIAL COMPLETE!** with a **CONTINUE OPERATIONS** button, allowing you to continue diving (`F`), surfacing (`F`), mining remaining deposits, selling ore, and restoring additional coral sanctuaries without resetting your company.
